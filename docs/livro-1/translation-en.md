## Places and terms

- **o Encante**: kept as *the Encante*, never "the Enchanted City". Glossed once in context.
- **encantado / encantada**: kept, italic on first use. Not "fae", not "spirit".
- **a Dona do Fundo**: *the Lady of the Deep*.
- **boto**: kept (*boto*, the Amazon river dolphin), plural *botos*.
- **Ana Jaci**: names untranslated; the Ana/Jaci split carries over as is.
